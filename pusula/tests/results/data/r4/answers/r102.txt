## Komutlar (UNVERIFIED, adım 2'deki gerçek komutla karşılaştır)

Paketleme (Windows):
```bat
"%UE_ROOT%\Engine\Build\BatchFiles\RunUAT.bat" BuildCookRun ^
  -project="C:\Proj\MyGame.uproject" -noP4 -platform=Win64 ^
  -clientconfig=Development -build -cook -stage -pak -archive ^
  -archivedirectory="C:\Builds\%DATE%"
```

Test (Windows). `Smoke` grubu örnek bir isimdir:
```bat
"%UE_ROOT%\Engine\Binaries\Win64\UnrealEditor-Cmd.exe" "C:\Proj\MyGame.uproject" ^
  -ExecCmds="Automation RunTest Group:Smoke;Quit" ^
  -ReportExportPath="C:\Reports\%DATE%" -unattended -nullrhi -nosplash -log
```
Mac/Linux'ta `RunUAT.sh` kullan.

Günlük zamanlama, Windows:
```bat
schtasks /create /tn "UE Nightly" /tr "C:\Proj\nightly.bat" /sc daily /st 03:00
```
Linux/Mac için `crontab` satırı: `0 3 * * * /home/me/nightly.sh`
